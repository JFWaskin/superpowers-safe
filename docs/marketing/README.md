## When to use which

- **Day 1** (right after the repo is ready): `github-settings-checklist.md` (set description, website, social preview, enable Discussions, pin to profile).
- **Week 1**: `awesome-list-pr.md` (PR to 3–5 curated lists).
- **Week 2**: `x-thread.md` and `reddit.md` (start a conversation, don't spam).
- **Week 3+**: `newsletter-pitch.md` and `influencer-DM.md` (targeted outreach).

## What to NOT do

- **Don't ask for stars.** "Please star if you like it" is fine; "Star this or I die" is not.
- **Don't spam issue trackers** of `obra/superpowers`, `anthropics/skills`, or other adjacent projects.
- **Don't post the same thread** to every subreddit in one day — accounts get flagged.
- **Don't create fake accounts** to upvote or fake engagement.
- **Don't claim this is "official"** or imply Anthropic/obra endorsement. It isn't. It's a community fork.
- **Don't over-promise.** "5 hard gates" is a specific, verifiable claim. Stick to specifics.

## How to measure

GitHub's traffic tab (Insights → Traffic) is the honest measure. If, two weeks after launch, you're seeing:

- 100+ unique visitors/day, and
- 30+ stars total, and
- 1+ issue or PR from someone who isn't you

…then the marketing is working. Adjust accordingly.
